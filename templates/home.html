Multimodal AI/ML Quality Pipeline Architecture

MODULE 1 • PYTORCH

Deep Image-Quality Classifier

Custom CNN architecture with transfer learning classifying scans into Good, Acceptable, Poor, and Unusable with calibrated softmax confidence scores.

MODULE 2 • OPENCV & NUMPY

Artifact & Motion Engine

Quantifies Laplacian blur variance, SNR/CNR foreground-background contrast, k-space motion ghosting, intensity bias fields, and FOV truncations.

MODULE 3 • SCIKIT-LEARN

Cohort Anomaly Detector

Isolation Forest & PCA dimensionality reduction across texture, spatial moments, and frequency embeddings to pinpoint corrupted or abnormal acquisitions.

MODULE 4 • PANDAS & STATS

Metadata & Bias Audit

Validates voxel spacing, magnetic field strength (1.5T vs 3T), scanner manufacturers (Siemens/GE/Philips), demographic sex/age balance, and test leakage risks.

MODULE 5 • ENSEMBLE

Research-Readiness Model

Fuses artifact penalties, metadata completeness %, outlier ratios, and scanner heterogeneity to categorize dataset as Ready, Requires Cleaning, or Not Ready.

EXPLAINABILITY & GENAI

Grad-CAM & Google Gemini

PyTorch Grad-CAM visual heatmaps overlaying degradation loci; Google Gemini translates structured JSON findings into scientific summaries and real-time answers.

🔬

Research-Support Scope & Distinction

NeuroData Quality AI is built specifically to automate dataset hygiene and quality assurance prior to training expensive deep learning models. It does not diagnose patients or replace clinical workflows. All core quantitative predictions are computed independently by PyTorch and Scikit-learn, with Gemini providing narrative synthesis.
